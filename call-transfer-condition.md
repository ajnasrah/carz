# Call Transfer action — field values

## "When to transfer" / scenario condition  (499 chars — field caps at 500)

Transfer to the office when the caller: asks about a specific vehicle (availability, price, miles, condition, features); wants one held; asks about payments, rates, money down, approval, which bank, or an application; asks what a trade is worth; wants to negotiate; has a complaint or trouble with a car they bought; asks for a person; sounds frustrated or repeats themselves; or asks anything you can't answer confidently. Transfer right away — don't ask permission or ask if there's anything else.

## "What to say before transferring" / Speak During Execution

Let me get you to the office — hang on just a second.

Keep it to that one line. It plays while the call is dialing, so anything
longer gets cut off. Works for a happy caller and an upset one alike, which
matters because complaints route through this same action.
