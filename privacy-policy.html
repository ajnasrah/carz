CarzInc.ai ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our automotive inspection and inventory management platform located at carzinc.ai (the "Service").

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent on the Service

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain our vehicle inspection and inventory management services

  • Generate analytics and insights about vehicle performance and sales

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest

By providing your mobile phone number, you consent to receive text messages from CarzInc.ai regarding:

11.2 Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies based on your account activity and preferences. Message and data rates may apply. We are not responsible for any charges incurred from your mobile carrier.

11.3 Opting Out of Text Messages

You may opt out of text messages at any time by:

  • Replying "STOP" to any message
  • Updating your notification preferences in account settings
  • Contacting support at support@carzinc.ai

Opting out of text messages will not affect your ability to use the Service but may impact timely notifications about important account activities.

11.4 Text Message Support

For help with text messaging, reply "HELP" to any message or contact our support team. We support all major U.S. carriers. International messaging may be limited.
