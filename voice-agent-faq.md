Add each as a SEPARATE FAQ entry. One question, one answer.

Answers are channel-neutral on purpose — they state the fact and say the
office handles the rest. They never say "hold while I transfer," because
the same knowledge base feeds both the voice agent and the SMS agent, and
each channel supplies its own handoff wording from its prompt.
