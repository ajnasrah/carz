# Carz Inc of Jackson — Voicemail Greeting

"Hey, you've reached Carz Inc of Jackson. We can't get to the phone right
now. We're open Monday through Saturday, nine to five. You can see
everything on the lot anytime at carz inc of jackson dot com. Leave your
name, your number, and what you're looking for, and we'll get right back
with you. Thanks for calling."

---

About 22 seconds at a normal pace.

Works whether you're closed, it's Sunday, or everybody's just on the phone —
"we can't get to the phone right now" covers all three, so you never have to
change it.

Notes:
* Record it in a real voice, not text-to-speech.
* Say the website naturally — "carz inc of jackson dot com" — don't spell it.
* No prices, payments, money down, or credit claims. A recorded greeting is
  advertising, and a down payment figure in an ad carries disclosure
  requirements a voicemail can't satisfy.
